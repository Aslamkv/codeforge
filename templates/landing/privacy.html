Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

The short version

We collect only what we need. We don't sell your data. We don't track you across the web. If you contact us, we store your message so we can reply. That's it.

What we collect

When you use codeforge.show, we may collect:

  • Contact form submissions — your name, email, and message, so we can reply.
  • Basic server logs — IP address, browser type, and pages visited. Standard stuff that every web server collects. We use it only for security and debugging.

We don't use third-party analytics trackers. We don't install cookies to follow you around the internet.

What we do with it

  • Reply to your messages.
  • Keep the site running and secure.
  • Nothing else.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We don't sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

The only exception: if we're legally required to disclose something (like a court order), we will — but we'll tell you if we're allowed to.

How long we keep it

Contact form messages are kept for as long as they're useful for the conversation. We clean up old data periodically. If you want your message deleted, just ask.

Your rights

You can:

  • Ask what data we have about you.
  • Ask us to delete it.
  • Ask us to correct it.

Just email us at hello@codeforge.show and we'll sort it out.

Changes to this policy

If we change something significant, we'll update the date at the top of this page. We won't bury important changes in legalese.

Questions?

Email us at hello@codeforge.show. We're humans. We'll reply.
